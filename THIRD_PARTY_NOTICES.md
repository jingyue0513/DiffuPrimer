# Third-party materials

The root MIT license covers original DiffuPrimer software and documentation.
It does not relicense external data, journal templates, publications, or third-party tools.

- NCBI RefSeq reference sequences and annotations retain their source attribution and applicable NCBI terms. Reference assembly: GCF_000001635.27; annotation RS_2024_02. Whole references and alignment databases are downloaded separately.
- PrimerBank source records retain their source attribution. The acquired partial snapshot and remapping manifests distinguish reported positions from computational weak supervision; no new access to a blocked service is required.
- Primer3, Bowtie, MMseqs2, qPrimer and PrimerCast retain their respective licenses. Third-party vendor directories and binaries are excluded from this Git release. Reproduction adapters and source/version records are included.
- OUP's authoring class and bibliography style, in manuscript source archives, retain their original embedded copyright and license notices. They are not covered by this project's MIT license.
- Third-party reference PDFs are excluded from the public code package. Bibliographic metadata and source links are retained.
- Trained weights and generated numerical benchmark outputs accompany the original implementation. Their source manifests preserve reference and supervision provenance.
